SUGGESTED TEST FLOW
--------------------
1. Customer app  - log in, pick a restaurant, add items to cart, check out.
2. Restaurant app - log in as the matching restaurant admin, accept the
   order in the Orders queue, move it to Preparing then Ready.
3. Delivery app  - log in as the rider, accept the delivery once it's
   ready, mark it out for delivery, then Delivered.
4. Customer app  - refresh order tracking to see the status update live,
   and leave a rating once delivered.

Other things worth trying:
- Add an item from Swindon Eats, then try adding one from Golden Wok -
  the app warns before clearing your cart to switch restaurants.
- Log in as superadmin@example.com and add a brand new restaurant (with
  its own admin login), then log in as that new admin to confirm it
  starts empty.
- Profile > Legal & Help / About Swindon Eats in the customer app.
- Cancel an order from the customer app before the restaurant accepts it.
- Restaurant admin's Profile tab > Store open/closed toggle - closing it
  blocks new customer orders.
